# The Plugin Glyph Atlas (v2)

Mark-level iconography reference from the industry's best-designed,
current-generation plugins — built as design inspiration for this
synthesizer's visual identity. **Open `plugin-glyph-atlas.html` in any
browser** (fully self-contained).

## v2 in one paragraph

97 authentic specimens cropped from 16 flagship plugins (Serum 2, Pro-Q 4,
Saturn 2, ShaperBox 3, DrumComputer, Effectrix, Massive X, Pigments, Myth,
Humanoid, Phase Plant, Hive 2, Ozone 11, smart:EQ 4, Vision 4X, Portal),
organized into six mark families: logotypes, waveform/curve iconography,
pictograms, navigation & badges, legends & indicators, and identity displays.
Corpus was chosen by design merit and currency first (user-approved vendor
list); imagery was pulled over open network from vendor sites and
PluginBoutique's standardized product galleries; every crop passed a
mark-level gate (no knobs, sliders or panel furniture). Gaps are recorded in
`provenance.json` rather than backfilled.

v1 of this atlas (open-source-skewed corpus, section-level crops, facsimile
plates) shipped earlier and was retracted after review; it is preserved in
git history along with its post-mortem. `community-wisdom.md` and `corpus.json`
are v1 research artifacts kept for the record. Tooling in `tools/`
(`crop.py`, `fetch2/3/4.py`, `build_atlas2.py`, `sheet.py`).
