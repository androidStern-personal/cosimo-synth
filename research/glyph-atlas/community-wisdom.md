## Design-approach folklore

- Community frames plugin UI as two camps: skeuomorphic 3-D bitmap renderings
  vs. flat vector design (KVR t=571256 GUI-framework thread; JUCE forum 8483).
- Recurring theme: eye candy < clarity; praised GUIs pair a strong aesthetic
  with legibility (KVR t=625585).
