Product and architecture
- The main Cmajor graph has a real ordered rack: Global Filter, Distortion, OTT, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Delay, Reverb.
- `sharedEngine.rackModOut` is connected to `rack.rackModIn`; 36 continuous rack destinations are live. Voice sources use Mean/Max reduction; macro/global sources do not.
- The full React synth UI exposes enable/bypass, grip-only single-commit drag/drop reorder, compact collapsed controls, and full editors for all eight modules.
- Distortion and Chorus reuse their established UI, OTT reuses the lab vocabulary, and Phaser/Delay have mutually correct Free/Sync controls.
- One descriptor table (`ui/shared/rack-parameter-descriptors.ts`) owns parameter ranges, labels, displays, and bindings. Approved rack art and wordmark fonts are vendored with attribution in `CREDITS.md`; instrument glyphs continue to use fontaudio.
- Reorders crossfade processed contribution through dry instead of muting the instrument. Time effects use constant-sum mix law; reverb has early energy and decay dependence; sync divisions are monotonic.
- The web pipeline still generates the real embedded Cmajor WebAssembly directly from `WavetableSynth.cmajorpatch`; no mock, localhost, runtime compiler, or external build service is shipped.

Deliberate clean break
- Canonical browser/adapter state is exactly `rack.v1 = {format, version, order, enabled}` and restores once per DSP session.
- The deprecated duplicate `chorusEnabled` endpoint and compatibility plumbing were removed. There is no Chorus migration or dual representation.
- Unpublished presets, DAW sessions, and automation tied to the old endpoint layout may break. Rationale is in `docs/ADR-015-clean-rack-state-and-structure-commit.md`.
