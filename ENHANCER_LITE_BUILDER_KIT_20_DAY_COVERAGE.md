# Enhancer Lite Builder Kit — Twenty-Day Coverage Check

Status: internal verification appendix for the plain-English daily schedule

This is not Andrew's morning checklist. It exists so agents can prove that every roadmap task has an owner, a time window, a finished result, and a clear reason to stop. It does not make vendor replies, legal approval, account approval, Apple access, hardware, or beta outcomes predictable.

Day numbers refer to August 28 through September 16, 2026. Work may be drafted early in parallel, but a task is not complete until its prerequisites and proof are complete. Any work before written commercial rights exist remains private, makes no customer-rights claim, and requires Andrew's explicit authorization if it changes code or external accounts.

## All seven release decisions

| Required decision | Scheduled proof | Failure result |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful customer rights | Day 5 written vendor/license/lawyer evidence | Move the combined launch |
| Exact product behavior | Day 5 approved product, supported DAW versions, and saved-setting/automation contract | Do not continue implementation |
| Customer source project works | Day 10 purchase-to-modification-to-update/recovery proof | Do not freeze beta build |
| Exact installer/source passes release checks | Day 11 one signed list covers the exact installer, immutable source release, prompt, instructions, and every tester file across both macOS versions, VST3/AU, Apple, store, and private feed | Do not start beta |
| Andrew and clean test installations accept the build | Days 11 and 19 exact-build Ableton/Logic and known-clean macOS 15/26 proof | Repair and retest; move launch |
| Ten-person test passes | Day 19 after seven full days on the unchanged signed file list, with support burden accepted for 50 buyers | Any required repair or unmanageable support burden moves launch |
| Final launch rehearsal passes | Day 19 unchanged files plus an unlisted real free delivery and paid purchase through live Polar, email/install/support/rollback proof | Publish nothing on Day 20 |

The later $49 decision is deliberately outside the twenty days and requires 60 days of customer evidence.

## Coverage verdict rules

- The task table must contain exactly 37 unique `BK-` rows.
- Every task must name a window, owner, proof, and stop condition.
- Day 20 is a launch day only when all Day 19 proof is for the same unchanged files and checksum used in beta.
- A legal answer, account approval, Apple result, hardware result, or beta outcome cannot be guaranteed by a schedule. Missing evidence moves the launch; it never becomes an assumed pass.
